# About Golden Legacy

**Who we are:**
Golden Legacy is a Dubai-headquartered corporate services firm focused on enabling founders and investors to set up and scale businesses in the UAE. The leadership includes Ms. Jasbir Kaur (Owner & Director) and Mr. Arvind Pal (CEO), with a cross-functional team of corporate services and PRO experts.

**Mission & Values:**
- Deliver premium end-to-end company formation and corporate advisory.
- Provide transparent pricing, regulatory compliance, and fast processing times.
- Prioritize client success and long-term relationships.

**What we offer:**
- Strategic guidance for mainland, free zone, and offshore formations.
- Banking introductions and account opening assistance.
- Compliance services: VAT, corporate tax, bookkeeping, and company secretarial.
- Residency and visa advisory including Golden Visa guidance.
