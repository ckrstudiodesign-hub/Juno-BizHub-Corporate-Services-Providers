# The Ultimate Guide to Company Formation in Dubai

Dubai remains the top destination for global entrepreneurs and investors. With its zero-tax policies in free zones, strategic geographic location, and world-class infrastructure, setting up a business in Dubai is highly lucrative. 

## 1. Mainland vs. Free Zone
Choosing between a **Mainland** and a **Free Zone** company is the first major decision.
- **Mainland:** Allows you to trade anywhere in the UAE and internationally. Requires Department of Economic Development (DED) approval.
- **Free Zone:** Offers 100% foreign ownership and tax exemptions, but limits trading within the local UAE market directly.

## 2. Process of Registration
1. **Choose your business activity:** Determine exactly what your company will do.
2. **Select a jurisdiction:** Mainland, Free Zone, or Offshore.
3. **Register the trade name:** Ensure it complies with UAE naming conventions.
4. **Apply for a trade license:** Submit your application to the relevant authority.
5. **Get your visas:** Apply for investor and employee visas.
6. **Open a corporate bank account:** We assist with seamless bank introductions.
